Mar to May 2026 · Code

Predict-then-optimize maintenance scheduling (NASA C-MAPSS FD001)

A BiLSTM-attention RUL model (test RMSE 15.90 cycles) feeding a mixed-integer linear program that assigns engines to overhaul slots under a 3-bay-per-cycle limit. Scheduling on point predictions left 11 engines failing before their slot; fixed, proportional and tiered buffers cut that to 7, 1 and 0, at about 11 cycles of useful life per engine.

Limitation: the buffers were tuned on the engines used to score them, so the zero-failure result would not hold for new engines. That led to the research above. The repository lists this and other problems at the top of the notebook.

Aug 2026 to present · Waylosa, an AI travel-planning startup

Leave planning as weighted interval scheduling

Formulated choosing leave days as weighted interval scheduling with two constraints and solved it exactly with dynamic programming. It matched brute-force search on 25 random cases and solves a full year in about 30 ms. A day-sequencing search (exhaustive to 7 stops, local search to 12) handles travel time, opening hours, daylight and fatigue.

Oct to Nov 2026 · IIT Roorkee capstone, simulated client

CloudServe: verification-first support system

Analyzed 500 historical tickets and 5 stakeholder interviews and found that 49% of escalated tickets were answerable from existing documentation. Built a pipeline that replies only after five checks and escalates the rest. 95% topic accuracy on unseen wording, confidence within 4.4 points of observed accuracy, 16% automated against a 65% target, with no private-data answers in validation runs.

Feb to Mar 2026 · Code

Explainable predictive maintenance (AI4I 2020)

Failure prediction on 10,000 machining records (3.4% failures) with a 70/15/15 split and no target leakage. Test PR-AUC 0.91 against 0.44 for logistic regression. A cost-optimal threshold chosen on validation data cost more on the test set, which shows that thresholds tuned on few failures need checking on new data.
