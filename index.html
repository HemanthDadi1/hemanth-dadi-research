What I work on

  • Operations research: scheduling, dynamic programming, heuristic search
  • Decisions under uncertainty: calibrated prediction intervals, risk-aware maintenance
  • Reliability and prognostics: remaining useful life, distribution shift
  • Analytics for service systems: turning messy operational data into a decision about what to build

Where this started

Last spring I built a maintenance scheduler for NASA turbofan engines that reported no in-flight failures. I had tuned the safety buffers on the same engines I scored it on, so the result would not hold for new engines. My current research asks the question that mistake raised: when predictions feed a schedule, how do we know the intervals are reliable for each engine, and not only on average?

Part I of that study is under review. Read the summary.
